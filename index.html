I’m a writer and a physician assistant student at Stanford School of Medicine. I write fiction and essays about bodies, care, and what comes next.

I studied English with a creative writing concentration at Rice University. My short fiction and essays have appeared in Subnivean, R2: Rice Review, Blood and Thunder Magazine, and the Rice Thresher. I attended the Tin House Summer Writing Workshop in 2024 and serve on the board of Anastomosis, Stanford’s medical school literary publication.

I’m currently seeking representation for SOLARIS, a New Adult science fiction novel about an academy student whose mother dies under strange circumstances at a research lab.
